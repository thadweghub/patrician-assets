# The Patrician — Logo Marks (reference for builders)

Final logo set, decided by TJ Nov 14, 2026. Three marks, all masters at
3200 × 3200 px in this folder (`logos/` in the patrician-assets repo).
Palette: the locked website palette — cream #F1E8DA, vanilla beige
#E4D4B9, beige #CDB493, brass camel #C09C61, milk chocolate #8A6642,
chocolate #6B4226, deep walnut #4B3019, espresso #0A0405.

RULES
- Every lockup carries "Pasadena, California" (TJ ruling, Nov 14, 2026).
- Any house depiction is based on the REAL property — the triple-arch
  loggia/courtyard with fountain (cf. photo P-35). Never invent a villa.
- Style: 1928 Hollywood, old-school old-world money, engraved heritage
  luxury, leaning toward the Haunted Mansion. No film strips or
  Hollywood clichés.

THE MARKS
1. **P monogram** — embroidery / small identity (hats, slippers), and
   the website favicon source.
   - monogram-p-transparent-3200.png (transparent background)
   - monogram-p-master-3200.png (on white)
   - monogram-p-lockup-pasadena-transparent-3200.png (monogram with
     THE PATRICIAN / HOLLYWOOD UNIVERSAL / PASADENA, CALIFORNIA beneath)
   - monogram-p-lockup-pasadena-master-3200.png (lockup on white)
2. **Circular seal** — souvenir / stationery / web seal; also the
   WhatsApp Business profile photo. Its scene is the most literally
   the real house: three arches, fountain, sunset through the middle
   arch.
   - circular-seal-pasadena-master-3200.jpg
3. **Haunted gate crest** — mood / web art. Its script lettering is the
   website wordmark font direction. Web use; muddies at small sizes,
   do not use small.
   - haunted-crest-pasadena-master-3200.jpg
